All lines by country

MKLines in North Macedonia

Frequent daily departures to the cities of western and central Macedonia. Tickets are sold at the station and on board.

ALLines to Albania

Via Struga and the Qafë Thanë border crossing to Elbasan, Tirana and the coast at Durrës. Peshkopi is served via Debar.

DELines to Germany

Direct coaches to southern and western Germany with stops in Skopje, Belgrade, Zagreb and Ljubljana. Reservation required at least 48 hours before departure.

CHLines to Switzerland

Our newest lines: Zürich and Basel twice a week, Bern and Geneva every Saturday. Two drivers on board, rest stop every 4 hours.

Bali Bus

Family-run bus company from Kičevo, connecting western Macedonia with the region and Western Europe since 1998.
