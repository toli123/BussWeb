About Bali Bus

A family company from Kičevo that has been moving people since 1998.

From one bus to four countries

Bali Bus was founded in 1998 by Bekim Baliu with one second-hand bus and a single line between Kičevo and Skopje. The idea was simple: reliable, affordable transport for the people of Kičevo and the surrounding villages.

In 2005 we opened our first international line to Tirana, and in 2011 the first coach left for Munich, following the many families from our region who live and work in Germany. Today our 18 coaches serve 21 destinations in North Macedonia, Albania, Germany and Switzerland.

We are still a family company. Every driver, mechanic and ticket clerk is part of the team that makes sure you arrive safely and on time.

What we stand for

Safety first

Every coach is inspected before each departure and serviced in our own workshop. Two drivers on all trips longer than 9 hours.

Reliability

We keep the timetable in summer heat and winter snow – 97 % of our departures in 2025 left on time.

Community

We sponsor local sports clubs and offer free transport for school excursions from the villages around Kičevo.
