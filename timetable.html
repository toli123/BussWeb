Tickets, discounts and luggage

Where to buy tickets

At the Bali Bus counter at the Kičevo bus station (05:00–21:00), on board for domestic lines, or reserve by phone / Viber for international lines.

Discounts

Children under 6 travel free, 6–12 pay 50 %. Students always get a return ticket with the way back free. Seniors 65+ 15 %, return tickets 10 % off.

Luggage

Two suitcases up to 25 kg each and one piece of hand luggage are included. Extra luggage 5 € per piece on international lines.

Reserve your seat

Call us or send a message on Viber / WhatsApp with your destination, date and number of passengers. We confirm within one hour during working time.
