Our services

More than a bus line – everything you need to travel or send something from Kičevo.

Intercity lines

Daily departures to Skopje, Tetovo, Gostivar, Ohrid, Struga, Debar, Bitola, Prilep and Makedonski Brod. No reservation needed.

International lines

Regular coaches to Albania, Germany and Switzerland with two drivers, Wi-Fi and rest stops. Reservation by phone or Viber.

Coach rental

Buses with 19, 35 or 55 seats with a professional driver for excursions, weddings, sports clubs and company trips – in the country and abroad.

Parcel service

Send packages and documents with our buses to any city on our network. Delivered the same day within Macedonia, in 1–2 days to Germany and Switzerland.

Student & commuter passes

Monthly passes for students and workers commuting to Skopje, Gostivar and Tetovo with up to 40 % savings.

Airport transfers

Minibus transfers from Kičevo to Skopje, Ohrid and Tirana airports, day and night, at a fixed price.

Your group, your schedule

Tell us where you want to go and when. We prepare a fixed-price offer that includes the driver, fuel, tolls and insurance.

  • Minibus 19 seats, midi-bus 35 seats, coach 55 seats
  • Air conditioning, Wi-Fi, TV and toilet on large coaches
  • Licensed for international transport (EU, Albania, Serbia)
  • Offer within 24 hours
