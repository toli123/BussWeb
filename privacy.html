Privacy & cookie policy

How this website uses your data and cookies.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Bali Bus DOOEL Kičevo, Bul. Osloboduvanje bb, 6250 Kičevo, North Macedonia (info@balibus.mk). We are the data controller for the personal data collected through this website.

2. Which data we collect

When you use the contact form we collect your name, e-mail address, phone number (optional) and the content of your message. We use this data only to answer your request and to prepare an offer or reservation. The data is deleted after 12 months unless a ticket or contract requires us to keep it longer.

3. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. We store only the following in your browser:

  • Necessary: a cookie called balibus_consent that remembers your cookie choice for 180 days, and
  • Preferences (only if you allow them): your chosen language (MK / SQ / EN), saved in local storage.

You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. If you choose "Necessary only", your saved language is deleted; the language you pick is then kept only in the page address (?lang=…).

4. Third-party services

The route map is provided by OpenStreetMap and the Leaflet library; map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers, which receive your IP address. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. All photos are hosted on this website. These providers may set their own technical cookies. We do not share your form data with any third party.

5. Your rights

Under the Law on Personal Data Protection of North Macedonia and the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to object to its processing. Write to info@balibus.mk and we will answer within 30 days.

6. Note

This website was created as a student project for the course Business Practice 2025/2026 at the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering, Skopje. Timetables, prices and team members shown are illustrative.
