Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before travelling with Bali Bus.

How do I buy a ticket?

For domestic lines you can buy the ticket at our counter at the Kičevo bus station or directly from the driver. For international lines please reserve at least 48 hours before departure by phone, Viber, WhatsApp or through the contact form and pick up the ticket at the counter.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, our counter at the station accepts Visa and Mastercard. On board only cash (MKD or EUR) is accepted.

How much luggage can I take?

Two suitcases up to 25 kg each and one piece of hand luggage are free. Extra luggage costs 5 € per piece on international lines. Bicycles are transported when space allows, for 10 €.

Where does the bus leave from?

All lines start at the Kičevo bus station, Bul. Osloboduvanje bb. Platform numbers are shown on the screen in the waiting hall. International lines depart from platform 1.

Do I need a passport for Albania, Germany or Switzerland?

For Albania an ID card is sufficient. For Germany and Switzerland you need a valid biometric passport. Please check the entry rules for your nationality before travelling.

Can I change or cancel my ticket?

Domestic tickets can be changed free of charge up to 2 hours before departure. International tickets can be changed once for free up to 48 hours before departure; cancellations are refunded 80 %.

Are pets allowed on board?

Small pets in a carrier are allowed on domestic lines free of charge. Guide dogs are always welcome. On international lines pets are not accepted.

Is there Wi-Fi and a toilet on the bus?

All international coaches have free Wi-Fi, USB charging and a toilet. Domestic buses have Wi-Fi and USB charging; there is no toilet on trips shorter than 2 hours.

How can I send a parcel?

Bring the parcel to the Bali Bus counter at least 30 minutes before departure with the name and phone number of the receiver. The receiver collects it at the destination station. Prices start at 150 MKD within Macedonia and 15 € to Germany or Switzerland.

Didn't find your answer? Contact us – we reply within one working day.
